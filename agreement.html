03 // The System

Three engines. One lead path.

Each channel does a different job at a different moment. Meta builds familiarity before the homeowner has a problem. ChatGPT Ads reach them while they are actively working out what to do. Reviews close the trust gap when they compare you to everyone else. All of it lands on the same funnel and the same lead path.

Engine 01

ChatGPT Ads

Decision Capture

Sponsored placements below relevant ChatGPT conversations, matched to the questions homeowners ask about leaks, storms, claims, aging roofs, and solar. Reaches them mid-decision, before they call three competitors off Google.

Engine 02

Meta Program

Demand Creation

Local, owner-led educational content on Facebook and Instagram: aging roof signals, claims education, solar coordination, project proof. Builds the retargeting pool and keeps Texas Roof Guardians the familiar name in your market.

Engine 03

Review Engine

Trust and Proof

An automated, policy-compliant review system: every eligible completed customer gets the same review invitation, every new review gets a factual owner-voice response, and your review profile grows in volume and recency month after month.

Why together
Run separately, each channel pays for its own learning. Run together, they share one funnel, one tracking layer, one retargeting pool, and one growing base of proof. The system compounds; isolated campaigns don't.
04 // Engine 01

ChatGPT Ads: reach the homeowner mid-decision.

Homeowners now ask ChatGPT the questions they used to type into Google: is this hail damage, should I file a claim, can this roof be repaired, what does replacement cost. A ChatGPT ad is a clearly labeled sponsored placement shown below relevant conversations. It does not change the answer, and delivery is guided by context, not exact keywords. What it does is put Texas Roof Guardians in front of the homeowner at the exact moment they are reasoning through a roof decision.

05 // Engine 02

Meta: build memory before the need.

Nobody plans to need a roofer. When the moment comes, the homeowner calls the name they already trust. The Meta program makes Texas Roof Guardians that name: short, local, owner-led educational content that earns attention without asking for a sale, then retargets the homeowners who engaged.

Campaign lanes

Four lanes, each with a job

  • Aging roof: repair vs replace, warning signs, the guide offer. Your evergreen lane, matched to what your best jobs actually are.
  • Roof + solar: detach and reset, warranty, one coordinated plan. The lane no competitor in your market is running.
  • Claims education: what to document, how the process works, calm and non-alarmist.
  • Retargeting: video viewers, engagers, site visitors, and leads not yet booked get proof, FAQ, and a low-pressure next step.
Creative system

Built to be sustainable

  • Phone-shot, owner-led video: local, real, and native to the feed. No agency gloss.
  • Batched production: one or two filming sessions produce a week of assets. You never owe five separate shoots.
  • Every asset gets one job: watch, save, download, call, or schedule. Never all five at once.
  • Winners scale on qualified actions and conversion data, not likes.
Your account, your data
Campaigns run in your Meta ad account. You own the account, the audiences, and every lead generated. Ad spend goes directly from you to Meta.
06 // Engine 03

The Review Engine: turn finished jobs into public proof.

Your 5.0 rating is one of your strongest assets. The problem with reviews at every roofing company is consistency: they get requested when someone remembers, and they stop the week things get busy. The Review Engine automates the whole loop through our platform, so it runs on every job, every time.

Collect

Automated requests

Every eligible customer

When a job completes, the customer automatically receives the same neutral review invitation by text or email. Consistent asks grow review volume and recency, the two signals that matter most.

Respond

Owner-voice responses

Every new review

Every new Google review gets a factual, local, owner-voice response. Active ownership of your profile, without keyword stuffing and without a review sitting unanswered for weeks.

Post

Profile activity

Ongoing presence

Review activity and responses keep your Google profile current and active, so the homeowner comparing three roofers at 9pm sees a company that is visibly alive and accountable.

Done by the rules
Every eligible customer gets the same invitation. A private feedback route is available to everyone, and it never blocks or filters the public review path. No gating, no selective asking, nothing that violates Google's review policies. Real reviews, systematically collected.

Condition satisfied: If two (2) or more Qualified Jobs are booked during the 30-Day Performance Trial, the remaining $3,000 becomes due immediately upon completion of the trial, and the Program will continue for the balance of the agreed 90-day term.

No recurring billing is created for the initial term, and any different payment terms recorded in the Additional Terms section of this agreement supersede this schedule.
